## Veldara (us) — mini-US aggregation

**Food core:** Heartland glacial grain. Dozens of market towns, each a classic **grain barony** (silo + mill + militia).

**Transport spine:** Big Heartland rivers running to the **East Gulf**; barges first, then a standard-gauge rail that won a 19th-century gauge war. Westreach only joins when rail tunnels/passes pierce **Highspine**.

**Aggregation story:**
1. River-mouth delta cities on the Gulf tax the grain flood and grow rich.
2. Inland baronies federate (or get hammered) into provincial grain boards — early federal habit: *local silos, common defense*.
3. Gulf + Heartland form the first real state; capital settles at **Aurel City**, the main confluence (navigation + politics).
4. Westreach port cities stay half-independent behind the mountains until federal money buys the passes — still culturally "coastal" and naval.
5. Northwood added thinly (timber/mining counties). Eastmarch settled as a **frontier belt** of new grain towns facing Korvath's towns on the same soil.

**Freeze:** A generation of Eastmarch wars ends in a surveyed line that satisfies no one — classic open-plain border. Modern Veldara is the federal bargain: Heartland votes + Gulf money + Westreach navy.

**Classical shadow:** Helioran law/ports via Westreach + Solara; local grain federalism is home-grown ([`10-classical-civilizations.md`](10-classical-civilizations.md)).

**Why we look like a mini-US:** coastal/port strip + mountain barrier + vast interior food basin + second warm-water gulf + an open land frontier.

## Korvath — the other silo empire

**Food core:** Same Eastmarch–plain system, continuing east into deeper Korvathi chernozem-analogue soils. Their baronies look like ours because the **dirt is the same**.

**Transport spine:** East-flowing rivers toward their own East Ocean / gulf outlets (passive margin ports). Rail centralized earlier under a more top-down monarchy → republic with militarized habits.

**Aggregation:** Grain boards unified under a court that treated the plain as one tax machine. Frontier culture faces *west* (us) the way ours faces *east*.

**Freeze:** Same Eastmarch wars; their textbooks say they lost a strip, ours say we held the line. Rivalry is structural: **two grain empires, one plain, weak natural border**.

**Great-power lean:** Kharzhan credits, doctrine, and spares — the continental patron that speaks "land war."

**Classical shadow:** Shan-Khar administrative-military habits via Kharzhan patronage ([`10`](10-classical-civilizations.md)).

## Doverin — the next catchment over

**Food core:** Southwestern basin on the far side of a gentle drainage divide from Veldara's Heartland. Good land, less of it.

**Transport spine:** Its own river to a smaller western/southern sea inlet; trade through Westreach passes when politics allow.

**Aggregation:** Same barony logic, fewer nodes → smaller country, denser towns, early habit of coalition diplomacy.

**Freeze:** Borders mostly follow the drainage divide (lazy, stable). That's why they're a **partner**, not a twin rival — different silo network, clear edge.

## Sereth — highland footnotes that became a state

**Food core:** Not grain empire territory. Pastoral valleys, rye/potato analogues, lake fish, later mining.

**Transport spine:** Valley roads; winter closures; a few rail spurs.

**Aggregation:** Clans/valley seats federate to keep Heartland tax farmers and Korvathi recruiters out. Identity = highland scarcity.

**Freeze:** Tree line and scarps make a **good-enough barrier**. Crises happen when someone wants a pass or a mine — not because either side needs the other's wheat field.

---

## Solara (Solmar island-continent)

**Food core:** Fertile eastern/southeastern lowlands + enormous **maritime protein** (fisheries). Never a Heartland-scale grain blob; wealth tilts to ports early.

**Transport spine:** Coastal shipping as the "highway"; interior rail secondary. Southern arc islands = waystations.

**Classical root:** Heir to the **Helioran Commonwealth** ([`10-classical-civilizations.md`](10-classical-civilizations.md)) — law, naval prestige, civic memory.

**Aggregation:** Port leagues → naval federation → federal maritime republic reclaiming Helioran legitimacy. Controlling **chokepoints and credit** beats controlling acreage.

**Freeze:** Ocean borders are easy; influence abroad is the real frontier. Becomes the maritime/tech great power — licenses, carriers, finance.

---

## Kharzhan State

**Food core:** Immense interior grain + river corridors on the craton; multiple hearth regions that were conquered into one tax space.

**Transport spine:** River trunk lines + rail land-bridges; passive west coast ports toward East Ocean.

**Classical root:** Claims succession from the **Shan-Khar Imperium** ([`10`](10-classical-civilizations.md)) — bureaucracy, census, “center endures.”

**Aggregation:** Classic continental pattern — unify the plains, then keep going until logistics snap. Militarized bureaucracy is how you run that many silos.

**Freeze:** Outer mountains on the far east/south are natural edges; the west is ocean. Abroad, clients (Korvath) extend reach without annexation.

---

## Nerath Compact (Farreach — Maravic War)

**Food core:** Windward wet coasts + irrigated valleys on their side of the suture range; early industrial coal/ore in the foothills.

**Transport spine:** Coastal rail + limited suture passes.

**Aggregation:** Trading cities federate into a compact; democratic institutions follow commercial grain/import dependence. **Helioran/Solara** civilizational lean.

**Freeze:** The suture orogeny is a real wall — until modern war makes passes and air matter. Fighting Tesen is fighting over **passes, foothill industry, and which way the grain and fuel move**.

---

## Tesen League (Farreach — Maravic War)

**Food core:** Rain-shadow flanks need irrigation empires — central control of water = central control of people.

**Transport spine:** Interior highways + pipeline logic later; fewer great ports.

**Aggregation:** League of irrigated provinces under a hard center. Authoritarian habit tracks water bureaucracy. **Shan-Khar/Kharzhan** civilizational lean.

**Freeze:** Same suture wars as Nerath; lean on Kharzhan for mass and spares.

---

## Iberon Union (on-stage, thin)

**Food core:** Mixed Mediterranean-analogue coasts on Solmar's periphery / nearby peninsulas.

**Role:** Broker and workshop — grows rich on **middleman trade** between Solara's high tech and everyone else's needs. Not a peer fighter; useful license/routing major.

**History in one line:** Port cities federate to stay ungobbled; neutrality-as-business model.
