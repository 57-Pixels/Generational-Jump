# Neighbors and Threats

> **Status:** framework — answer the key questions, then replace the starter suggestions with canon.
> Detailed conflict scenarios live in `threat-analysis/scenarios/`; this doc establishes *who exists*. Keep the actors here and the war-gaming there.

## Key questions to answer

1. **Who shares a border with us, and what is each one's attitude?** For every neighbor: hostile, rival, neutral, friendly, or ally? Attitudes can be asymmetric with capability — a hostile weak neighbor matters less than an ambivalent strong one.
2. **Which neighbor could actually end us?** Distinguish existential threats from harassment threats. The force is built for the former; the latter must be handled with what's left over.
3. **What does the dangerous neighbor's military actually look like?** Size, equipment generation (by analogue), doctrine, and — most importantly — its *weaknesses*. We can't out-spend them, so designs must attack weaknesses.
4. **Are any threats non-state?** Insurgency, cross-border militias, organized smuggling that funds hostile actors?
5. **Who is behind whom?** Which great power backs which neighbor, and what does that backing include — money, weapons, intelligence, or troops?
