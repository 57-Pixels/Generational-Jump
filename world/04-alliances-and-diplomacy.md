# Alliances and Diplomacy

> **Status:** working design — who sells to us vs who fights beside us.

## Baseline

- **No automatic defense treaty** with Solara or anyone else. Materiel help possible; troops not guaranteed. Plan to fight with what we can make and stockpile for the opening months.
- **Doverin:** real partner — co-production and interoperability soft-requirement (ammo, datalinks, fuels).
- **Preferred path:** license + domestic build for high-volume items; imports as bridges.

## Suppliers

| Source | What we get | Strings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Solara** | Best tech, licenses, machine tools, high-end aerospace | Political approvals, end-use monitoring |
| Non-aligned advanced exporters (Iberon and others) | Near-current systems, faster tech transfer | Fewer strings — sweet spot for many programs |
| Surplus / legacy market | Stopgaps while lines spin up | Dries up in every crisis |
| **Kharzhan** | Generally not a supplier to us | Arms Korvath instead |

## Escalation

Kharzhan has signaled that deep strikes into Korvath's core cross a line. Interdiction near the border is different from hitting their capital — design programs with that ceiling in mind.

## License clauses that matter

Wartime-continuation and spare-part production rights in every major license (see rifle worked example).
