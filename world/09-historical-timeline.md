## Climate / map eras

| Era ID | When (Earth-analogue) | Map / climate | Human note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lgm` | ~26–19 ka BP | Max ice; sea −120 m; East Gulf shelf exposed | Sparse foragers; shelf and ice-edge peoples |
| `termination` | ~19–12.9 ka BP | Melt pulses; rising seas; unstable | Migrations; drowned camping grounds |
| `younger_dryas` | ~12.9–11.7 ka BP | Cold/dry snap (esp. north) | Northern stress; southern continuity |
| `early_holocene` | ~11.7–8 ka BP | Warming; seas nearly modern by end | Broad-spectrum foraging → early cultivation experiments |
| `mid_holocene` | ~8–4 ka BP | Optimum then regional aridification pulses | Farming spreads into Heartland / Kharzhan plains / Farreach wet coasts |
| `late_holocene` | ~4 ka BP – 1500 CE | Near-modern coasts | Grain baronies → kingdoms → early empires |
| `early_modern` | ~1500–1800 | Little Ice Age–analogue optional | Gunpowder empires; Solmar naval rise begins |
| `industrial` | ~1800–1945 | Modern seas; fossil energy | Rail, steel; Solara & Kharzhan crystallize as great powers |
| `cold_war_analogue` | ~1945–1991 | Bipolar competition | Proxy habits; legacy force generations |
| `present` | ~1991–2025+ | Maravic War era | Veldara still on 80s–90s kit → generational jump |

Generator today implements **`lgm`** and **`present`** snapshots; other eras can interpolate later.

## Civilization milestones (working)

| When | Event |
| --- | --- |
| ~11–9 ka | Independent agricultural starts in at least two hearths (northern river plains + one Farreach wet coast) — Earth Neolithic *pattern* |
| ~6–4 ka | Irrigation / grain-board polities on Heartland-analogue and Kharzhan rivers |
| ~3–2 ka | Classical-scale states; Highspine toll powers; first durable Eastmarch frontier wars |
| ~1000–1500 | Maritime leagues on Solmar; continental consolidation on Kharzhan |
| ~1500–1800 | Gunpowder + ocean sailing → Solara’s naval edge; colonial/trading footprints (footnotes) |
| ~1800–1900 | Rail gauge wars on Aurelian; Veldara federal bargains (Gulf + Heartland + Westreach passes) |
| ~1900–1945 | Industrial total wars (offstage detail later); force structures that become “legacy” |
| ~1945–1990s | Bipolar Solara–Kharzhan order; Veldara/Korvath client-lean patterns |
| ~1990s–2010s | Veldara stagnates on late-Cold-War kit while politics underfunds modernization |
| ~2020s | **Maravic War** on Farreach (Earth Ukraine-era *lessons*, fictional belligerents) |
| Now | Veldara **Modernization Act** / generational jump (the series) |
