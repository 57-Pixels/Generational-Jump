# Legendary Figures — Classical Age

> People who bent history hard enough that **every modern schoolkid** still knows the name.
>
> **Rule:** Earth supplies the *job* (conqueror who dies young; lawgiver; canal-tamer). This world supplies the *person*. No Alexander, Caesar, Qin Shi Huang, Ashoka, or Cleopatra on the map — only their roles.
>
> Language/culture stems: [`10-classical-civilizations.md`](10-classical-civilizations.md)  
> Dated spine: [`09-historical-timeline.md`](09-historical-timeline.md)

Working names. Keep the role if you rename.

## Legend tiers (how big the myth is)

| Tier | Meaning | Use in series / sim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S** | World-bending; taught everywhere both sides of the ocean | Episode epigraphs, academy mottos, war-room jokes |
| **A** | Civilizational ceiling for one stem (West *or* East) | National myth; officer exams quote them |
| **B** | Regional immortal; one theater or one institution | Local place names, doctrine footnotes |
| **C** | Semi-legendary / disputed | Useful as fog — scholars argue, publics don’t care |

## Western pantheon (Helioran stem)

| Figure | Tier | Earth *role* | Legendary deed (this world) | Modern echo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Caelus the Far-Sailor** | A | Homeric founder / Odysseus–Aeneas mix | Charts the West Ocean littoral; founds the first lasting port-league myth | Solara academies; “Caelian wake” = correct coastal approach |
| **Orsania Philarch** | A | Lawgiver (Solon / Twelve Tables) | Writes the Commonwealth charter — contracts, citizen rights, jury forms | “Orsanian law” = basic civil rights language in Veldara & Solara |
| **Tiber Ambros** | **S** | **Alexander the Great** | In ~10 years: Solmar → Westreach → Heartland fringe; dies young; **Ambrosian Partition** among generals | Diadochi-style rival leagues in Westreach memory; *the* schoolbook map of overreach |
| **Lysara of Halcyon** | A | Cleopatra / dynastic-navigator queen | Holds a successor kingdom by navy + grain diplomacy after the Partition; last Helioran “queen of two coasts” | Iberon romance literature; Solara soft-power kitsch |
| **Marcian Vale** | **S** | Caesar / foundational imperator | Ends civil wars; builds the port-road spine; killed on the Senate quay | Republican ghost in Solara politics; “Vale’s quay” as assassination proverb |
| **Helena of the Archives** | A | Constantine / cultural consolidator | Binds clerical archive to the state; seeds continuity cults | Lateran-type enclaves; archival clergy footnotes |
| **Cassian of the Twin Harbors** | A | Hannibal-scale operational legend (West) | Crosses the Highspine with a land army no one thought could; never takes the Solmar core — but forever proves “plains can threaten ports” | Korvath and Veldara both teach the campaign; different morals |
| **Rhem the Burned** | A | Last defender (Stilicho–Aetius mix) | Holds the arc through ash years and sea peoples; dies with the outer provinces | Tragic textbook staple; ash-decade poems |
| **Admiral Vespera** | B | Themistocles / naval decisive | Wins the choke-strait battle that saves the Commonwealth’s first naval monopoly | Solara fleet toast; Iberon pilots’ lore |
| **Quillmaster Edran** | B | Aristotle / school-founder (secular) | Systematizes Helioran natural philosophy and rhetoric for officers | Academy curriculum fossils |

**Ceiling myth (West):** Tiber Ambros. If a kid only knows one classical name, it’s his — and the Partition that followed.

## Eastern pantheon (Shan-Khar stem)

| Figure | Tier | Earth *role* | Legendary deed (this world) | Modern echo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shan of the First Canal** | **S** | Yu the Great / culture hero | Tames the floods; invents the grain-board idea; half history, half myth | Every canal opening ceremony; Kharzhan origin pageants |
| **Kharran the Examiner** | A | Institutional founder (exam state) | Invents the exam bureaucracy — merit ladder over blood alone | “Kharran ladder” = modern officer/tech schools East *and* Korvath |
| **Iron Prefect Soru** | **S** | Qin Shi Huang / legalist consolidator | Standardizes weights, script reform, brutal canal corvée; hated and indispensable | Dual-script literacy politics; “Soru’s rod” = harsh reform |
| **Empress Li-Shen** | **S** | Expansionist unifier (Han–Wu mix) | Reunifies after long fracture; garrisons toward East Ocean; poetry’s golden age | Classical poetry canon; women’s officer academies claim her |
| **General Wei Dongar** | **S** | **Alexander-adjacent land conqueror** | Generation of campaigns across the steppe margin into Aurelian’s eastern plain; never permanently holds the Heartland | Korvath officer toasts; East’s “one man bent the map” myth |
| **Ashara of the Twin Rivers** | A | Ashoka / conqueror-turned-ethicist | Wins a bloody reunification war, then publishes the **Asharan Edicts** limiting massacre and binding census mercy | Reformers East and West quote the edicts selectively |
| **The Silent Mandate** | A | Philosophical pivot (personage disputed) | Ends a dynasty without a massacre (or so the texts say) | Every later reformer’s favorite ghost |
| **Marshal Ken-Yara** | A | Yue Fei / loyal general betrayed | Saves the center, then is recalled and killed by court intrigue | Officer honor codes; tragic ballads in Tesen & Kharzhan |
| **Scholar-Admiral Zhen** | B | Zheng He–analogue (rare East naval) | Leads the one classical-era ocean expedition that sticks in memory — then the court abandons the sea | Explains why East was late to blue-water prestige vs Solara |
| **Census-Lord Mava** | B | Administrative immortal | Completes the first empire-wide headcount that actually feeds armies | Bureaucracy’s patron saint; dry joke in sim UI later |

**Ceiling myths (East):** Shan (founding water), Soru (standardization trauma), Li-Shen (reunification glory), Wei Dongar (operational genius). Different classrooms emphasize different ones.

## Series / sim usage notes

- **Epigraphs:** S-tier only, unless the episode is regional.
- **Doctrine docs:** cite Orsanian law, Kharran ladder, Ambrosian Partition — not Earth analogues.
- **Naming lock:** when a working name is replaced, update this file and [`10`](10-classical-civilizations.md) in the same commit.
- **No Earth proper names** in on-screen dialogue or map labels. Roles stay in *these* tables for writers only.
