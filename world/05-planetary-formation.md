## Planet parameters (Earth-twin)

Same physics stack as Earth so 2025 tech and climate intuition transfer:

| Parameter | Value | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size / gravity / atmosphere | ≈ Earth | Familiar aviation, ballistics, weather |
| Day / year | ≈ 24h / ≈ 365d | Familiar seasons and logistics calendars |
| Axial tilt | **~23°** | Seasons; climate belts |
| Rotation | Same sense as Earth | Coriolis → gyres and storm tracks |
| Oceans | ~71% surface (land ~29%) | Maritime powers and SLOCs are real; Earth-comparable land budget |
| Plate tectonics | **Active** | Mountains, trenches, arcs, rifts, earthquakes, volcanoes |

No magic. Continents move. Mountains rise at collisions and arcs; they erode when quiet. Rivers run downhill to the nearest ocean or endorheic basin. Deserts sit where Hadley cells and rain shadows say they should.

**Definitions (canon):** a **continent** is a **continental plate** (or major continental fragment). A **landmass** is continuous dry land and may contain multiple continents (Farreach = Nerath + Tesen). Present **land ≈ 29%** of surface. Principles: [`12-worldbuilding-principles.md`](12-worldbuilding-principles.md). Generator: [`../maps/generator/deeptime/`](../maps/generator/deeptime/).

## Reference campaign vs new seeds

The named features below (Aurelian, Highspine, East Gulf, …) describe the **Generational-Jump reference campaign**. New stories may run deep-time from another seed; the *processes* stay mandatory, the *coastlines* become seed artifacts.
## Deep time (how the present map happened)

Told as a short geologic story — enough to justify shapes, not a textbook.

### 1. Assembly (~1.2–0.6 Ga)

Several Archean–Proterozoic cratons (old, stable continental cores) drifted and sutured into two large ancestral landmasses:

- **Proto-Aurelian–Kharzhan** — a broad northern megacontinent (think one big "north world" assembly).
- **Proto-Farreach** — a southern assembly, long separate.

Solmar's core was a smaller continental fragment on the western edge of the northern assembly, already rift-prone.

### 2. Breakup of the northern megacontinent (~180–80 Ma)

A mantle plume and back-arc extension opened a **north–south rift** that became the **East Ocean**.

- East side of the rift kept the thicker, older interior → became **Kharzhan** (huge craton + long accretionary margin on its *outer* east and south, not on the young rift coast).
- West side became **Aurelian**.
- The rift shoulders are **passive margins** today: broad continental shelves, few volcanoes, sediment-built coasts — *not* young Andean mountains on those shores.

This is why Aurelian and Kharzhan "face" each other across a wide ocean with relatively gentle facing coasts and big river mouths — Atlantic-analogue logic, not Pacific-analogue logic.

### 3. West Ocean subduction (ongoing ~100 Ma → present)

Oceanic plate under the **West Ocean** dives eastward beneath Aurelian's western edge **and** wraps around Solmar.

Consequences that **must** appear on maps:

- **Aurelian West Cordillera (Highspine):** Andean/Cordilleran-type fold-thrust belt + volcanic arc just inland of the West Ocean coast. Steep west-facing slopes, rain-intercepting peaks, rain shadow immediately inland.
- **Offshore trench** (subtle on atlas maps as a darker bathymetric line parallel to Westreach).
- **Solmar** sits on a fragment above the same broad subduction system: western Solmar is mountainous/arc-volcanic; eastern Solmar is a gentler back-arc / remnant-ocean shelf. The **southern Solmar archipelago** is a volcanic island arc where the slab rolls back — Japan/Aleutian *logic*, not random dots.

### 4. Aurelian interior & the East Gulf (Cenozoic)

After breakup, Aurelian was a continent with:

- Active **west** margin (subduction → Highspine).
- Passive **east** margin (old rift → East Ocean).

In the mid–late Cenozoic, **oblique extension and sediment loading** opened / deepened a large embayment on the southeastern Aurelian shelf — the **East Gulf**:

- Not a random cartoon bite: a failed or partial arm of post-rift extension + soft sediment coast (Mississippi/Gulf-analogue *process*).
- Wide, shallow shelf; hurricane/typhoon-analogue fetch; major deltas from Heartland rivers.
- Korvath occupies the continent east of Veldara's Eastmarch, still on Aurelian crust — the border is political across continuous geology, not a plate edge.

**Sereth** sits on a **Proterozoic highland remnant** in the north — old, cold, uplifted, glacially sculpted — not a young volcanic chain. That is why the northern border is highland without a trench.

**Doverin** sits on the southwestern Aurelian block, same plate as Veldara; border is political.

### 5. Farreach (south) — collision in progress

Farreach never fully joined the northern continents. In the last ~40 Ma, two Farreach plates converged:

- **Nerath block** and **Tesen block** collide along a **central suture orogeny** (Himalaya/Zagros-*analogue* logic: high interior range, seismicity, rain shadows on both flanks depending on monsoon direction).
- That suture is why Farreach has a serious mountain spine and why the Maravic War has brutal highland and desert-flank theaters — geology first, politics second.
- Farreach straddles tropics to subtropics: Hadley-cell deserts inland of orographic wet coasts where winds hit the suture range.

### 6. Ice ages (last ~2.5 Ma)

Repeated northern glaciations scoured **Northwood** (lakes, disordered drainage, thin soils) and left the Heartland with thick glacial/outwash and loess-friendly soils — the grain belt exists *because* of ice, not because we wanted farmland on the map.

## Present plate sketch (what the world map must imply)

```text
WEST OCEAN                 EAST OCEAN
(subducting  →)            (passive / wide)
        │                         │
   SOLMAR plate        AURELIAN plate     KHARZHAN plate
   (fragment)          west: cordillera   west: passive
                       interior: plains   interior: craton
                       SE: East Gulf      east/south: older
                       north: old highland accretionary belts

              FARREACH LANDMASS (south)
              Nerath plate | SUTURE | Tesen plate
```

Farreach is **one landmass, two continents** (two continental plates). That is the living example of continent ≠ landmass.
## Climate physics (non-negotiable belts)

With ~23° tilt and Earthlike circulation:

| Latitude band (approx.) | Expect |
| --- | --- |
| Equator ±10° | Wet tropics where oceans/ITCZ dominate; not desert unless rain-shadowed |
| ~15–30° | Desert / arid belts on west coasts and interiors (Hadley subsidence) — **unless** a warm gulf or monsoon breaks the pattern |
| Mid-latitudes | Storm-track wet west coasts *windward of cordilleras*; continental interiors drier/seasonal |
| High latitudes | Cold, glaciated north on Aurelian/Kharzhan; Farreach is mostly too far south for huge ice sheets except highland glaciers |

**Ocean gyres:** West Ocean and East Ocean each get subtropical gyres. Warm western-boundary currents can humidify east coasts of continents (Gulf Stream / Kuroshio *logic*). Aurelian's Gulf Coast stays humid subtropical because of a warm gulf + onshore flow — not because we painted it green.

**Rain shadow:** Westreach wet marine coast → Highspine wrings moisture → immediate lee toward western Heartland is drier steppe before the plains pick up continental rainfall patterns further east.

## Hydrology rules

- Major rivers rise in Highspine, Sereth highland, or Eastmarch uplands and reach West Ocean or East Gulf — they do **not** cross the Highspine westward against the drainage.
- Endorheic pockets only where rain shadow + basin topography allow (small OK; don't invent a second Caspian without cause).
- East Gulf deltas are muddy and shifting — bad for deepwater ports unless dredged; true deepwater sits on rockier Westreach inlets and selected Gulf hard points.

## What this forbids on maps (common fake-map sins)

- Mountain ranges parallel to a **passive** East Ocean coast with no collision/arc story
- Volcano icons sprinkled inland with no arc
- Rivers that climb or split continents without drainage logic
- Circular continents or perfectly symmetric "four petals"
- Tropical rainforest glued to a west-coast desert latitude without a monsoon exception written down
- Island chains that aren't arcs, hotspots, or drowned ridge fragments
